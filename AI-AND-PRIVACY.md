# Reviewing Windows AI features and data collection

Apply this guide to the installed Windows edition, build and account. The inventory can identify registered apps and visible optional features. For a setting the collector cannot see, ask the owner to confirm its state in Settings. Record unavailable and organization-managed controls explicitly.

| Owner goal | What to check | Decision to record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Remove the standalone Microsoft Copilot app | Match the exact current-user package to its entry under Settings > Apps. Confirm that Windows offers Uninstall. Microsoft 365 Copilot and features inside Edge or Office require separate review. | Ask whether the owner wants to remove this specific app. Explain the loss of the standalone entry point and verify a reinstall source for the same app. [Microsoft app removal guide](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/uninstall-or-remove-apps-and-programs-in-windows) |
| Stop Recall from saving future snapshots | On a supported Copilot+ PC, check Settings > Privacy & security > Recall & snapshots. An installed feature alone cannot establish whether saving is enabled. Snapshots are stored locally for the user. | Ask whether to turn off saving. Separately ask about existing snapshots or removing the optional feature. Treat deletion as irreversible and verify the documented effects of feature removal before proposing it. [Microsoft Recall guide](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/ai/ai-features/retrace-your-steps-with-recall) |
| Reduce Windows diagnostic data sent to Microsoft | In Windows 11 open Settings > Privacy & security > Diagnostics & feedback. In Windows 10 start at Settings > Privacy. Check whether optional diagnostic data is enabled and whether policy controls it. | If enabled and editable, ask whether to turn off optional diagnostic data. Required diagnostics can still be sent. Review deleting previously collected diagnostic data as a separate decision. [Microsoft diagnostics guide](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/privacy/diagnostics-feedback-and-privacy-in-windows) |
| Reduce data collection while continuing to use Copilot | Confirm the app and profile in use. Review its optional diagnostic data, history and sync controls individually. | Ask which data the owner wants to stop sending or syncing. Account history deletion requires its own decision. [Microsoft Copilot privacy FAQ](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot/privacy-faq-for-microsoft-copilot) |

For each selection, record the observed state, evidence source, desired state, affected account or device, and recovery limits. A missing package match or inaccessible feature list leaves the finding unknown. Keep private history and snapshot contents out of the review.
